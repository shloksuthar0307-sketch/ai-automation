Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 2026 • Compliant with Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023 (India)

1. Introduction & Scope

I value your privacy and believe in complete transparency. This privacy policy outlines how I, {{ developer_name }} ("Sitewright"), collect, process, and protect your personal information when you use this website (sitewright.ai) or submit a project quote request.

2. Data Collected & Grounds for Collection

I only collect personal information that you voluntarily choose to provide via the contact and quote forms. This includes:

  • Identity & Contact Details: Your name, email address, and/or WhatsApp/phone number.
  • Project Details: Business name, project requirements, budget plan selection, and design notes.
  • Technical Information: IP address for server security and atomic brute-force rate limiting (not tied to marketing profiles).

The lawful basis for processing this data is your explicit consent when requesting a quote and my legitimate interest in communicating regarding your web development project.

3. Purpose of Processing

Your details are used strictly for:

  • Reviewing your website requirements and drafting tailored project proposals.
  • Communicating with you directly via email, phone, or WhatsApp regarding your inquiry.
  • Maintaining website security and preventing automated spam or malicious bot submissions.

I do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to any advertisers, data brokers, or third parties.

4. Data Storage & Retention

Your data is stored in secure, access-controlled databases. Inactive inquiries are purged periodically. If we do not proceed with a project, you can request immediate removal of your record at any time.

5. Your Rights Under the DPDP Act (India)

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you hold the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Access: Request a summary of the personal information stored about you.
  • Right to Correction: Request updates or corrections to outdated or inaccurate information.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): Request the permanent deletion of your contact data from the records.
  • Right of Grievance Redressal: Direct any privacy-related concern to me personally.
